Public institutions

Governments, public schools, public libraries, and qualifying charities may use the TownLight Public Institution Exception for their own deployments. The exception allows qualifying public institutions to keep modifications to their own deployments private instead of offering that source code to website users under AGPL section 13. Contractors qualify only under the conditions stated in the exception.

Businesses and service providers

You may use, modify, host, and sell services around Townlight under the AGPL. The public-institution exception does not give vendors a blanket right to run a closed-source commercial Townlight platform.
