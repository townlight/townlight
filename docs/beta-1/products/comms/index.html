Workflow

How it works

Each public item follows an explicit path from source selection to a durable public record. Staff approval stays human and tied to the exact revision reviewed.

  1. Source
    Select a supported source and verify that it is public, current, and eligible for publication.
  2. Draft
    Draft in plain text with source references attached. Editing clears prior approval.
  3. Review
    Preview the exact revision, access report, and source snapshots before sign-off.
  4. Approve and schedule
    Record the approval hash, actor, and time; schedule portal or email delivery.
  5. Publish and preserve
    Capture receipts, corrections, withdrawals, source snapshots, and a Records archive entry.

Staff and resident workflows

Communications capabilities

Drafting and review

  • Source-backed draft review with public eligibility checked at runtime.
  • Exact-revision previews and explicit human approvals.
  • Edit invalidation, stale-review prevention, and optimistic revision checks across two-PC work.

Publishing and history

  • Scheduled publication to the resident portal and email.
  • Correction and withdrawal with ordered publication history.
  • Source snapshots and a Records archive containing published revisions and receipts.

Resident experience

  • Approved article list and detail in the resident portal.
  • Subscriber confirmation, preferences, and unsubscribe.
  • No resident access to staff drafts or private source data.

Evidence and controls

  • Source references retain kind, identifier, revision, digest, and public URL.
  • Approval hashes and content digests support exact-revision accountability.
  • Visible queued, delivered, and failed states; destructive actions require confirmation.

Interface outline

Screens, without mock screenshots

The staff workspace and resident portal provide different views of the same publication.

Staff Communications workspace

Saved draft list and filters, source picker, plain-text editor, exact-revision preview, Access review, approval, scheduling, delivery status, correction and withdrawal, and Records archive.

Resident portal

Approved article list and article detail, plus subscription confirmation, preferences, and unsubscribe. Staff drafts and private source data are not exposed.

Responsibility

Roles and handoffs

The design reuses the existing staff shell and existing administrator, city-staff, and clerk grants. No new authentication provider is introduced.

Author or editor

Selects sources, prepares plain text, and resolves source or access issues beside the relevant field.

Reviewer or clerk

Reviews the exact revision and access report, records explicit approval, and schedules the approved item.

IT or city operator

Uses the Windows suite and existing city configuration, including encrypted SMTP settings. There is no package or pip installation path for the city operator.

Resident subscriber

Confirms opt-in, manages preferences or unsubscribes, and reads only approved public articles.

Deployment

IT configuration

  • Platform: Windows suite integration; the Communications source is under development.
  • Identity: native staff authentication and existing administrator, city-staff, and clerk grants.
  • Runtime boundary: native staff gateway under /desktop/v1/communications; fixed allowlisted browser commands, with no arbitrary URL access.
  • Data: durable Communications domain with immutable revisions, source snapshots, approvals, schedules, delivery state, and Records archive.
  • Email: existing city SMTP transport and encrypted settings, with a separate Communications outbox and visible uncertain-send state.
  • Installation: included in the signed Windows suite installer.

Scope boundaries

Limits and non-claims

Not included

  • No social channels.
  • No AI automation.
  • Staff approval required before scheduled publication.
  • No campaign or advocacy content.
  • No legal advice or certified translations.

Human control

Staff approval remains explicit. Missing, private, or stale sources block approval. Manual source-backed content does not require AI. Subscriptions are opt-in, and future mail excludes unsubscribed recipients.

Parks integration

Staff can select eligible public Parks sources for a Communications draft.

Installed Windows verification

After the desktop closed, the scheduled article went live, its email reached the test mailbox, and Records archived the published revision. Release acceptance.
